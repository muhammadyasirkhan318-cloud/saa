About Siratic Academy

We are committed to making quality education accessible to everyone. Combining traditional Islamic knowledge with modern academic excellence in a warm, supportive learning environment.

Our Story

Founded in 2018 by a team of passionate educators and scholars who wanted to make quality education accessible to everyone.

Siratic Academy began with a simple yet powerful vision: to provide authentic, personalized, and high-quality education that nurtures both the mind and the soul. We combine traditional teaching excellence with modern technology, offering one-on-one live classes in Quran, Mathematics, English, and Programming. Our dedicated teachers focus not just on academic success, but on building confidence, character, and a love for learning.

Today, we proudly serve thousands of students across more than 50 countries, helping children and adults achieve their educational and spiritual goals.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, personalized, and transformative education in Quran, Mathematics, English, and Programming that nurtures both academic excellence and personal growth for learners of all ages worldwide.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted global online academy, empowering students with strong academic foundations, Quranic knowledge, and essential life skills — preparing them to succeed in this world and the Hereafter.

Our Scholars & Teachers

Carefully selected experts committed to academic excellence and student success across all subjects.

Our teaching faculty comprises highly qualified professionals with strong academic backgrounds and extensive teaching experience. From certified Quran scholars with Ijazah to subject experts in Mathematics, English, and Programming, every instructor is chosen through a rigorous selection process.
